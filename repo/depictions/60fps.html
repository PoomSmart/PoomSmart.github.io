Compatibility

  • Compatible with iOS 7.0 +

Description

  • Enables rear 60 fps video that firmware already has but hides: 720p60 on iPhone 4s (H4ISP, through iOS 9.3.6) and 1080p60 on iPhone 5s (H6ISP, through iOS 12.5.8).
