Compatibility

  • Compatible with iOS 7.0 +

Description

  • Enables hidden Photos internal (prototype) settings.


    Open Photos and touch anywhere in the app. A round PT button appears at the bottom left, above the tab bar. Tap PT to open internal settings, then tap Done to save. The button hides after about 3 seconds of inactivity.

Changelog
